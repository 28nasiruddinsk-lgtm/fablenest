What Author Branding Means

Author branding is the look, voice and story that readers connect with your name. It covers things like your bio, colours, fonts and logo, and it makes your books, website and social media feel like they belong together.

Parents, teachers and librarians usually choose children’s books from authors they recognise. A clear, consistent brand makes you easier to remember and easier to recommend.

A brand does not have to be complicated. For most authors a short story about who they are, a few colours and fonts, and a simple logo is enough to start.

Who this is for

  • Authors with more than one book
  • Authors starting a website or social media pages
  • Authors who visit schools and events

Step by step

What is author branding?

It is the look, voice and story that make you recognisable as an author, such as your colours, fonts, logo, bio and the way you talk to readers.

Why does a children’s author need a brand?

Parents, teachers and librarians choose books from authors they recognise and trust. A consistent brand across your books and pages makes you easier to remember.

What do I get at the end?

A brand kit with your author story, logo or mark, colour palette, fonts and bios, ready to use on your website, covers and social media.

Can the brand match my existing books?

Yes. We can build on the style of your current books so everything feels like it belongs together.
