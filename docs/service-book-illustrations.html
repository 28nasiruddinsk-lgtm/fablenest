How Children’s Book Illustration Works

Illustrating a children’s book means designing the characters and drawing the scenes that tell the story alongside the words. It is done in stages so you can guide the look before the artwork is finished.

In a picture book the illustrations show things the words do not say, such as how a character feels or what is happening in the background. Good illustration also keeps a child interested from one page to the next.

Style matters as much as skill. A gentle story suits soft watercolour, while a funny one may suit bold, bright colour. We help you choose before the full artwork begins.

Who this is for

  • Authors who write but do not draw
  • Authors who have a rough sketch of their idea
  • Authors who need a cover illustration
  • Authors planning a series with the same characters

Step by step

  1. 1

    Your hand-drawn idea

    You can sketch the idea on paper, as rough as you like. It only shows us what you imagine.

  2. 2

    Line art

    Our artist redraws the idea with clean lines, and you can ask for changes before colour is added.

  3. 3

    Colour

    After the lines are approved the artist adds colour, mood and light.

  4. 4

    Final illustration

    Details, shading and background finish the picture, ready for print and screen.

Key terms, in plain words

Line art
The outline drawing of a picture before colour is added.
Character sheet
A page showing one character from several angles so it looks the same on every page.
Spread
Two facing pages illustrated as one picture.
Bleed
Extra picture beyond the page edge so no white shows after trimming.

Questions Authors Ask

Quick, honest answers about book illustrations. Can’t see yours? Send us a message and we’ll reply.

Ask a question
What does a children’s book illustration service include?

Character design, illustrations for each page or spread, cover artwork, rounds of revisions, and final files prepared for print and ebook.

Can I choose the illustration style?

Yes. We show you styles that suit your story, such as watercolour or bold digital colour, and you approve one before the full artwork begins.

How many illustrations does a picture book need?

It depends on the length and format of your book. We review your manuscript and recommend how many spreads or pages to illustrate.

Will I see sketches before the final artwork?

Yes. You review rough sketches first and can request changes before they become finished illustrations.
