How much does it cost to publish a children’s book?

Most self-published picture books cost between about $1,500 and $8,000 all-in, and illustration is usually more than half of that. A very lean book can cost a few hundred dollars if you draw it yourself and use print-on-demand. A fully illustrated, professionally edited and designed book with a launch plan can pass $10,000.

The honest answer is a range, because three choices move the price more than anything else: who illustrates it, how many pages it has, and how many printed copies you want. The sections below show each stage so you can set a budget you can defend.

Children’s book illustration cost

Illustration is the largest line. Marketplace guides put a full-page interior illustration at roughly $35 to $350, and a 24-page picture book illustrated by a freelancer has been reported at $1,540 to $4,950. Per-page rates vary with the style (simple spot art costs less than full-colour spreads), the number of characters, and the rounds of changes you need.

A fixed per-page price is easier to budget than an hourly rate, and character design is often charged once and reused on every page. If you plan a series, ask whether the characters can be reused at no extra cost.

Editing, cover design and layout

Picture books have few words, so editors often charge a flat fee instead of a per-word rate. A flat fee of a few hundred dollars is common, and a few providers charge more for very short texts because every word matters.

The cover is the sales page of your book. Illustrated covers by professional artists have been quoted between $500 and $3,000. Layout (placing text and pictures, setting bleed and trim, and exporting print-ready PDFs) is smaller but easy to get wrong, so it is worth paying for.

ISBN, copyright and registration

You need an ISBN for each format (paperback, hardcover, ebook) if you want your book to be tracked by retailers and libraries. Rules and prices differ by country, so use the official agency for yours.

Printing and distribution

Print-on-demand (Amazon KDP, IngramSpark) has no upfront cost, and the printing fee is taken from each sale. Guides put the unit cost at roughly $2 to $7 for a picture book, with hardcovers at the top of that range. Amazon pays a 60% royalty on the list price after the print cost, so a book priced at $14.99 with a $4 print cost earns about $4.99 per sale.

Offset printing (a run of 500 to 1,000 copies) lowers the per-copy price but needs several thousand dollars upfront and storage space. For a first book, print-on-demand is the lower-risk way to start.

Marketing and launch

Many first-time authors spend $50 to $200 on a launch and then wonder why nobody finds the book. A better budget puts money into the things that keep working: a clear author website, a short book trailer, a press release to local and parenting media, and a simple social plan.

If money is tight, do the website and one press release first, then add the trailer when you have your first reviews.

How to lower the cost without lowering the quality

  • Keep the page count to 24 or 32; printers work in multiples of eight.
  • Choose one illustration style and keep backgrounds simple.
  • Agree on the number of revision rounds before work starts.
  • Buy ISBNs and design files once and reuse them across formats.
  • Start with print-on-demand and order a small offset run only after you see demand.

Children’s Book Questions, Answered

Quick, honest answers about cost to publish a children’s book. Can’t see yours? Send us a message and we’ll reply.

Ask a question
How much does it cost to publish a children’s book?

Most self-published picture books cost between about $1,500 and $8,000, with illustration the largest cost. A lean DIY book can cost under $1,000, and a fully illustrated, designed and promoted book can pass $10,000.

How much does a children’s book illustrator cost?

Public guides put a full-page illustration at about $35 to $350, and a 24-page picture book at roughly $1,500 to $5,000 with a freelance illustrator. Style, page count and revisions change the price.

Can I publish a children’s book for under $1,000?

Yes, if you illustrate it yourself or use simple art, do your own layout and use print-on-demand, which has no upfront printing cost. Expect to trade some polish and time for the lower price.

How many pages should a picture book have?

Most picture books have 24, 32 or 40 pages. 32 is the standard because printers bind pages in multiples of eight, and it keeps illustration cost predictable.

Do I need an ISBN?

You need one for each format if you want retailers and libraries to track your book. Amazon KDP can assign a free one for print, but then Amazon is listed as the publisher, so many authors buy their own.

Who keeps the rights to the book?

You do. Self-publishing keeps the copyright with you, and a written agreement with any illustrator or editor should say who owns the artwork and text before work begins.
