In short

Fablenest Publishers helps authors turn their ideas into professionally made children’s books. Our team combines editorial, design, illustration and publishing knowledge, and works closely with each author from the first idea to the finished book.

Our aim
Books children enjoy and authors are proud of

Every children’s book begins with an idea, a spark of imagination, or a story waiting to be shared. We believe those ideas deserve the opportunity to become something extraordinary.

Our focus is helping authors turn their creative visions into professionally crafted children’s books. Through thoughtful design, engaging illustrations, editorial expertise, and publishing support, we help bring stories from the page to the hands of young readers.

Whether you’re a first-time author or an experienced storyteller, we provide creative solutions and professional guidance to help you navigate the publishing journey with confidence.

From the earliest stages of development to the final published book, we’re here to help you create stories that entertain, inspire, and stay with readers long after the last page.

How We Work With Authors

We are a team that helps authors make children’s books. We work on the editing, pictures, design and launch together, and we keep the author involved at every stage.

Our starting point is always the author’s idea. We ask about the story, the child it is for and what you hope the book will do, and then suggest the services that fit. Nothing is added that you do not need.

Because the same team handles the words, the pictures and the cover, the finished book feels like one piece of work rather than several parts joined together.

Who this is for

  • Authors who want a guide, not just a printer
  • People who prefer to be involved in the creative choices
  • Authors who want text, pictures and cover to match

Key terms, in plain words

Manuscript
The written text of your book before it is edited, illustrated and laid out.
Brand kit
A short guide to an author’s colours, fonts, logo and bio so everything looks consistent.
Proof
A preview copy of the book checked for mistakes before it is published.

Questions Authors Ask

Quick, honest answers about working with us. Can’t see yours? Send us a message and we’ll reply.

Ask a question
Who is Fablenest Publishers for?

We work with authors of children’s books, from first-time writers with a single story to experienced authors building a series.

How does the process work?

It starts with a conversation about your book and goals. Then we agree the services you need, work through each stage with your feedback, and prepare the finished book for publication.

Will I be involved in the creative decisions?

Yes. You approve the key steps, such as the illustration style, the cover and the final layout, so the book stays true to your vision.

What makes a good children’s book?

A clear, engaging story, pictures that support the text, an age-appropriate length and language, and a professional finish in editing, layout and cover.

How can I contact the team?

Use the contact form on this website. Tell us a little about your book and we will get back to you.
