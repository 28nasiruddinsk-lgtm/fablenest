What a Press Release Does for a New Book

A press release is a short, formal announcement of news. For a book it tells journalists, bloggers and librarians that the book exists, what it is about and why it is worth mentioning.

Editors receive many announcements, so a release should be clear in the first lines, factual and easy to copy into an article. It usually includes a headline, the key facts, a quote from the author and contact details.

A release gives your news the best chance of being noticed, but it cannot guarantee coverage. That is why a good media list matters as much as the writing.

Who this is for

  • Authors with a launch date
  • Authors with an award, event or milestone to announce
  • Authors who want local media attention

Key terms, in plain words

Media list
A list of journalists, bloggers and outlets that might cover your news.
Headline
The first line of a release that sums up the news.
Boilerplate
A short standard paragraph about the author at the end of a release.

Questions Authors Ask

Quick, honest answers about press release. Can’t see yours? Send us a message and we’ll reply.

Ask a question
What is a press release for a book?

A news announcement about your book, written in a standard format so editors, bloggers and librarians can quickly see what the book is and why it matters.

When should I send a press release?

Usually around your launch date, so coverage can appear when readers can actually buy the book. We help you choose the timing.

Who receives the press release?

We build a list of outlets and contacts that cover children’s books, such as parenting sites, local media and library or school newsletters.

Does a press release guarantee coverage?

No. It gives your news the best chance of being noticed and picked up, but editors decide what they publish.
