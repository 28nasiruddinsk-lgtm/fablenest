What Book Publishing Involves

Book publishing is the work between a finished manuscript and a book a reader can buy: editing, cover design, interior layout, file preparation and setting up the book on selling platforms.

Many authors think of publishing as one step, but it is really several. Each one affects how professional the book looks and how easily a reader can find and enjoy it.

Publishing a book yourself on a platform gives you control, but it also means the quality of the editing, cover and layout is up to you. That is the part our service takes care of.

Who this is for

  • Authors with a manuscript ready to publish
  • Authors who want to self-publish with professional help
  • Authors who have never used a publishing platform before

Step by step

  1. 1

    Review the manuscript

    We read your text and agree what editing and proofreading it needs.

  2. 2

    Edit and proofread

    The text is edited for clarity and checked for spelling, grammar and consistency.

  3. 3

    Design the cover

    A cover is designed that reflects the story and is easy to read at thumbnail size.

  4. 4

    Format the interior

    Text and pictures are laid out for print and for ebook readers.

  5. 5

    Publish

    The finished files are uploaded to the platform you chose and the book page is set up.

Key terms, in plain words

Self-publishing
Publishing a book yourself, so you keep control of the rights and decisions.
Interior formatting
Laying out the text and images on each page so the book reads well in print and on screen.
Proofreading
The last careful check for spelling, grammar and punctuation errors.
Trim size
The width and height of the printed book.

Questions Authors Ask

Quick, honest answers about book publishing. Can’t see yours? Send us a message and we’ll reply.

Ask a question
What is included in your book publishing service?

Manuscript preparation (editing and proofreading), book cover design, interior formatting, and support with submitting your book to the publishing platform you choose.

I have never published a book. Can you guide me?

Yes. We explain each stage in plain language, so you can make informed decisions without needing any publishing experience.

Do you help with the book cover?

Yes. Cover design is part of the service. We create a cover that reflects your book and works well as a small thumbnail online.

Which platforms can my book be published on?

We can prepare your book for the major platforms, including Amazon Kindle, Barnes & Noble, IngramSpark and Lulu, depending on your goals.

Can I publish a book that is already written but not edited?

Yes. Manuscript preparation covers editing, proofreading and content refinement, so your text is polished before it is formatted.
