What an Author Website Does

An author website is a site you own where readers can learn about you, see your books and find where to buy them. Unlike social media, you control it, and it stays useful for years.

A good author site is simple. It has a page for each book, a short bio, a way to contact you and a sign-up form for news. Search engines also use it to understand who you are and what you write.

Most visitors will use a phone, so the site needs to load quickly and be easy to read on a small screen.

Who this is for

  • Authors with a published or soon-to-be-published book
  • Authors who want readers to find them online
  • Authors who run events or school visits

Questions Authors Ask

Quick, honest answers about author website. Can’t see yours? Send us a message and we’ll reply.

Ask a question
Do I need an author website?

It is not required, but it is the one place online that you fully control. It helps readers, parents and schools find your books, and gives you somewhere to send people from social media.

What pages will my website have?

Usually a home page, an about page, a page for each book with buy links, a news or blog page, and a contact page with a newsletter sign-up.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Most visitors use a phone, so every page is designed to work well on small screens first.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We set it up so you can add a new book or a news post without needing a developer, and we show you how.
