How to Publish a Children’s Book, Step by Step

Publishing a children’s book means taking an idea through writing, editing, illustration, design and printing, and then telling readers it exists. Many first-time authors are unsure where to begin, so here is the path most books follow.

A children’s picture book is a different job from a novel. The words are few, so every one counts, and the pictures carry half of the story. That is why editing, illustration and layout are planned together rather than one after the other.

You can do the steps yourself, hire different people for each, or use one team for all of them. The advantage of one team is that the text, pictures and cover are made to match.

Who this is for

  • First-time children’s authors
  • Authors with a finished manuscript
  • Writers with only an idea
  • Authors planning a series
  • Teachers, parents and grandparents who write for children

Step by step

  1. 1

    Shape the story

    Decide who the book is for, the age group and the one idea a child should remember. Most picture books are short and have a clear beginning, problem and ending.

  2. 2

    Edit the text

    Edit and proofread the manuscript so every word earns its place. Read it aloud, because children’s books are often heard before they are read.

  3. 3

    Plan and create the pictures

    Choose an illustration style and plan the pages. Sketches come first, then colour, so changes are easy to make early.

  4. 4

    Design the cover and layout

    Design a cover that works as a small thumbnail online, and lay out the pages with text and pictures balanced.

  5. 5

    Publish in print and as an ebook

    Prepare the files for the platforms you choose, such as Amazon Kindle, IngramSpark or Lulu, and set up the book page.

  6. 6

    Tell readers about it

    Share the launch with a website, social media, a short trailer and a press release so the right people can find the book.

Key terms, in plain words

Picture book
A children’s book where illustrations and text work together on every page, usually written for ages up to about eight.
Print on demand
A way of printing a book only when someone orders it, so no stock has to be stored.
Spread
Two facing pages of a book, which are often illustrated as one picture.
ISBN
A number that identifies a particular edition of a book for retailers and libraries.

Questions Authors Ask

Quick, honest answers about working with us. Can’t see yours? Send us a message and we’ll reply.

Ask a question
What does Fablenest Publishers do?

We help authors publish children’s books. That includes editing, cover design, illustration, formatting, and the marketing around the book, such as a trailer, an author website and social media.

Do I need a finished manuscript to start?

No. You can come with a finished manuscript, a draft, or only an idea. We explain the options and help you decide the next step.

Can I choose only the services I need?

Yes. Some authors want the full publishing journey, others only an illustrator, a cover, or a launch campaign. Each service can be used on its own.

Where will my book be sold?

Books can be prepared for the main retailers and print-on-demand platforms, including Amazon Kindle, Barnes & Noble, IngramSpark and Lulu.

Do I keep the rights to my book?

Authors stay in control of their work. We give you a clear written agreement before any work begins, so you know exactly what you own and what we are doing.

How do I get started?

Send us a short message through the contact form with a few lines about your book. We will reply to talk through your goals and suggest the right services.
