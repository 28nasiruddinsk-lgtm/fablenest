The short version

To publish a children’s book you finish and edit the manuscript, plan the illustrations, design and format the book, get an ISBN, choose how you will publish (self, hybrid or traditional), print or upload it, and then market it. Most of the work, and most of the cost, is in the illustrations and the design.

The order below is the order that saves money. Fixing a problem in the text costs almost nothing; fixing it after the artwork is finished can mean paying for every page again.

The eight steps

  • Write and edit the manuscript. Picture books are short, so every word counts. Get a children’s book editor or at least two trusted readers before you spend on art.
  • Plan the pages. Decide the page count and lay the story out page by page (a storyboard or "dummy") so you know how many illustrations you need.
  • Commission the illustrations. Choose an illustrator whose style fits the story, agree the scope, number of pictures and ownership in writing, then approve sketches before final colour.
  • Design the cover and interior. The cover sells the book; the interior needs correct trim size, margins and bleed for the printer.
  • Get an ISBN and register the book. Each format (paperback, hardcover, ebook) needs its own ISBN.
  • Choose your publishing route (compared in the next section).
  • Proof, then print or upload. Always order a printed proof; colours on a screen and on paper differ.
  • Launch and market. Plan this before the book is finished: reviews, a website, social posts, school and library outreach.

Self-publishing, hybrid or traditional: which should I pick?

The right route depends on how much control you want, how much you can spend, and how fast you want the book out.

Whichever route you choose, read the contract for who owns the copyright, who owns the illustrations, and how you can end the agreement.

Where your children’s book can be sold

  • Amazon (print and Kindle) through Kindle Direct Publishing, which has no upfront cost to upload.
  • IngramSpark, which distributes to bookstores, libraries and online retailers worldwide.
  • Your own author website, where you keep the full sale price and the customer details.
  • Direct sales at schools, events and readings, often the most reliable channel for a first picture book.

Mistakes that cost authors the most

  • Paying for illustrations before the text is final.
  • Not agreeing in writing who owns the artwork and what it may be used for.
  • Skipping the printed proof and finding colour or margin errors after printing.
  • Choosing a page count the printer cannot bind (picture books usually print in multiples of 8 or 16 pages; confirm with your printer).
  • Planning marketing only after the book is on sale.

How long does it take?

It depends mostly on the illustrations and on how quickly feedback is returned. A short picture book can take a few months from a finished manuscript when decisions are made promptly, and much longer when the artwork is revised many times. Ask any illustrator or publisher for a written schedule before you start.

Questions Authors Ask

Quick, honest answers about how to publish a children’s book. Can’t see yours? Send us a message and we’ll reply.

Ask a question
How do I publish a children’s book?

Finish and edit the text, plan the pages, commission and approve the illustrations, design the cover and interior, get an ISBN, choose self, hybrid or traditional publishing, order a printed proof, then print or upload and market the book.

Can I publish a children’s book without an illustrator?

Yes, if you illustrate it yourself. Otherwise picture books need illustrations, so most authors hire an illustrator; text-only chapter books for older children do not.

Is self-publishing a children’s book worth it?

It can be if you want control and speed and can fund the illustrations and marketing. Sales depend heavily on your own promotion, so plan for that from the start.

Do I need an agent to publish a children’s book?

Not to self-publish or use a hybrid publisher. Most traditional publishers only accept submissions through a literary agent.

Do I need an ISBN for a children’s book?

You need one to sell through most bookstores and distributors. Each format (paperback, hardcover, ebook) needs its own. Amazon can provide a free one for books you sell only there.

How many pages is a children’s picture book?

Most are 32 pages, though 24 or 40 pages also occur. Printers bind in set page multiples, so confirm with yours.
